## The Concept

This lesson is different from every lesson before it. There's no single new concept to learn. Instead, this is a **guided project** — your chance to take *everything* you've learned and build a complete web page from scratch.

Think of it like learning to cook. You've spent weeks learning to chop, sauté, boil, and season. Now it's time to make the whole meal.

Here's what your landing page will include:

- **A navigation bar** — links that let visitors jump to different sections
- **A hero section** (`header`) — the big, bold intro with a name, tagline, and call-to-action
- **An about section** — a photo, bio paragraph with **bold** and *italic* emphasis
- **A services section** — an unordered list of what the copywriter offers
- **A work section** — an ordered list of top achievements with results
- **A contact section** — links to get in touch (at least one opening in a new tab)
- **A footer** — copyright info

You'll notice some new tags here: `<nav>`, `<header>`, `<section>`, and `<footer>`. These are called **semantic elements** — they work exactly like containers (similar to `<div>`), but their names tell the browser *what kind of content* is inside. A `<nav>` holds navigation. A `<header>` holds introductory content. A `<section>` groups related content. A `<footer>` holds closing info. They make your code more readable and more accessible.

There's no single "right answer" here — the page should feel real. And remember: this page is **YOUR foundation**. In the CSS course, you'll come back and transform it into a polished, professional landing page.

## The Syntax

Here's a complete example. We'll use a fictional copywriter named **Sarah Chen** so you can see how all the pieces fit together — but when it's your turn, feel free to customize it however you like.

```html
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html>
<head>
    <title>Sarah Chen | Freelance Copywriter</title>
</head>
<body>

    <!-- Navigation -->
    <nav>
        <a href="#about">About</a>
        <a href="#services">Services</a>
        <a href="#work">Work</a>
        <a href="#contact">Contact</a>
    </nav>

    <!-- Hero Section -->
    <header>
        <h1>Sarah Chen</h1>
        <p><em>Freelance Copywriter</em></p>
        <p>I write words that <strong>convert visitors into customers</strong>.</p>
        <a href="#contact">Hire Me</a>
    </header>

    <!-- About Section -->
    <section id="about">
        <h2>About Me</h2>
        <img src="https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1573497019940-1c28c88b4f3e?w=300" alt="Sarah Chen smiling at her desk with a laptop" width="300">
        <p>
            I'm a <strong>freelance copywriter</strong> with 5 years of experience
            helping brands tell their story. I specialize in
            <em>website copy, email campaigns, and SEO content</em> that
            doesn't just sound good — it <strong>drives results</strong>.
        </p>
    </section>

    <!-- Services Section -->
    <section id="services">
        <h2>My Services</h2>
        <ul>
            <li>Website Copy & Landing Pages</li>
            <li>Email Marketing Campaigns</li>
            <li>Blog Posts & SEO Content</li>
            <li>Social Media Copywriting</li>
        </ul>
    </section>

    <!-- Selected Work Section -->
    <section id="work">
        <h2>Selected Work</h2>
        <ol>
            <li>Redesigned landing page copy for TechStart — <em>increased conversions by 40%</em></li>
            <li>Email sequence for GreenLeaf Organics — <em>generated $12k in first week</em></li>
            <li>Blog strategy for CloudBase — <em>tripled organic traffic in 3 months</em></li>
        </ol>
    </section>

    <!-- Contact Section -->
    <section id="contact">
        <h2>Let's Work Together</h2>
        <p>
            Ready to level up your copy?
            <a href="mailto:sarah@example.com">Send me an email</a>
            or find me on
            <a href="https://twitter.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Twitter</a>.
        </p>
    </section>

    <!-- Footer -->
    <footer>
        <p>&copy; 2026 Sarah Chen. All rights reserved.</p>
    </footer>

</body>
</html>
```

Notice how the page flows naturally from top to bottom — navigation, hero, about, services, work, contact, footer. That's how real landing pages are structured. Each section uses an `<h2>` heading, keeping the hierarchy clean under the single `<h1>`. The navigation links use `#about`, `#services`, etc. — these are called **anchor links** that jump to the matching `id` on the page.

This is a **complete, valid HTML5 page**. It has no styling yet — it looks plain in the browser. But every element is in the right place, every image has alt text, and every section serves a clear purpose. **Structure first, style later.** That's professional web development.
